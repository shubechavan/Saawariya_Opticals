Our Story

Who We Are

A Legacy of Clear Vision

Founded over 15 years ago by Jaisingh Yadav and Bhupsingh Yadav, with Vinay Yadav as Co-Founder, Saawariya Opticals began as a small eyewear shop with a big vision — to make quality eye care accessible to everyone in Bhayander.

Over the years, we have grown from a single outlet to two strategically located stores, serving thousands of satisfied customers across Bhayander West, Bhayander East, and surrounding areas including Naigaon and Mira Road.

Our commitment to combining the latest optical technology with genuine personal care has made us the most trusted name in eyewear in the region. Every customer who walks through our door is treated like family.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

At Saawariya Opticals, our mission is to provide high-quality eyewear and eye-care solutions that enhance vision and style. We strive to combine professionalism, trust, and customer care with the latest optical technology to help our customers see and look their best.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and innovative eyewear destination in the region, where every customer finds not just perfect vision — but also a style statement that reflects who they are. We envision a future where quality eye care reaches every corner of our community.
