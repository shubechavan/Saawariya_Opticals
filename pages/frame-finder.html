What's your face shape?

Take a photo looking straight at the camera, then compare to the shapes below. If unsure, oval is the safest bet!

Oval

Face is longer than wide with a slightly narrower forehead and jaw

Round

Width and length are nearly equal with soft, curved edges

Square

Strong jaw, broad forehead, roughly equal width and height

Heart

Wider forehead and cheekbones tapering to a narrow chin

Diamond

Narrow forehead and chin, wide cheekbones at the middle

Oblong

Long and narrow face, forehead and jaw are similar width
