Why It Matters

Regular Eye Tests Are Essential

Vision problems often develop gradually. A regular eye check helps detect issues early and ensures you always see clearly.

Early Detection
Detect conditions like glaucoma, cataracts, and macular degeneration before symptoms appear.
Accurate Prescription
An outdated prescription strains your eyes. Keep it current for optimal comfort and clarity.
Screen Eye Strain
Digital eye strain from screens is common. We assess and recommend the right protection.
Children's Vision
Vision problems in children affect learning. Annual checks are vital for academic performance.
The Examination

What to Expect

1

Visual Acuity Test

2

Refraction Assessment

Reading Your Prescription

Understanding Your Results

Here's a simple guide to understand your eye prescription.

Term Full Name What it Means
SPH Sphere The lens power needed to correct nearsightedness (–) or farsightedness (+)
CYL Cylinder The lens power for astigmatism correction
AXIS Axis The orientation of the cylinder lens (0–180 degrees)
ADD Addition Additional power for reading (used in bifocals/progressives)
PD Pupillary Distance Distance between the centres of your pupils — critical for accurate lens placement

Our optometrists will explain your prescription in detail and recommend the most suitable lens options.
