Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer home delivery for glasses?
Yes, we do offer delivery for orders placed through our website. Standard delivery is available across Mumbai Metropolitan Region. Free delivery on orders above ₹999.
How long does it take to get prescription glasses made?
For standard prescriptions, we can typically have your glasses ready in 24–48 hours. For specialized lenses (high index, progressive, photochromic), it may take 3–5 days. We will inform you of the exact timeline at the time of order.
Is the eye test really free?
Yes! Eye testing is completely free at both our outlets. No appointment needed — just walk in. Our certified optometrists will conduct a comprehensive examination including visual acuity, eye pressure check, and prescription assessment.
Do you repair glasses frames?
Yes, we offer frame repairs including nose pad replacement, hinge repair, and general adjustments. For frames purchased from us, basic adjustments are complimentary. Visit either outlet for an assessment.
Can I bring my prescription from another doctor?
Absolutely! You are welcome to bring any valid prescription (typically up to 1–2 years old) and we will make your glasses accordingly. Our opticians will guide you on the best lens options for your prescription.
What warranty do you offer on frames and lenses?
All frames carry a minimum 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Branded frames (Ray-Ban, Titan, Oakley, etc.) come with their respective brand warranties. Lenses are covered for coating peeling within 1 year under normal use conditions.
